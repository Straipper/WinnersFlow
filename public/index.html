03 — STRATEGIES

Support. Resistance. Flow. Confirmation.

DT FLOW combines key levels, market flow and price confirmation into repeatable frameworks designed for historical testing and evaluation.

01

Breakout + Flow Confirmation

Price reaches resistance while buy-side activity increases. DT FLOW waits for price confirmation before treating the move as a setup.

02

Breakout + Retest

Instead of chasing the first move, watch whether price returns to the broken level and confirms the retest.

03

5-Bar Acceptance

A custom DT FLOW framework that watches repeated closes above a defined resistance zone before treating acceptance as confirmed.

04

Absorption Setup

Watch for aggressive buying or selling being absorbed around support or resistance before price confirms the setup.

05

Delta Divergence

Compare directional flow with price movement to identify potential confirmation or divergence around key levels.

06

Stacked Imbalance

Stacked directional pressure can highlight areas where flow may be confirming a move through an important level.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is DT FLOW? +
DT FLOW is a trading intelligence terminal combining stock prints, options flow, support and resistance, price action and structured setup context.
Does a large print mean the stock will go up? +
No. Flow is confirmation and context, not a guaranteed prediction. DT FLOW combines flow with support or resistance and price confirmation so users can evaluate a setup rather than blindly follow a transaction.
Is the data in this demo live? +
The landing-page examples are demo data used to demonstrate the interface. The production terminal will connect to market-data providers through a backend so real-time flow and market data can be delivered securely.
Can I use DT FLOW on mobile? +
Yes. The interface is designed mobile-first while retaining the denser terminal experience on larger screens.
Does DT FLOW provide financial advice? +
No. The product is intended as a research and market-context tool. Users remain responsible for their own trading decisions.
