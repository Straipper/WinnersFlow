FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is WinnersFlow? +
WinnersFlow is a trading intelligence terminal combining stock prints, options flow, support and resistance, price action and structured setup context.
Does a large print mean the stock will go up? +
No. Flow is confirmation and context, not a guaranteed prediction. WinnersFlow combines flow with support or resistance and price confirmation so users can evaluate a setup rather than blindly follow a transaction.
Is the data in this demo live? +
The landing-page examples are demo data used to demonstrate the interface. The production terminal will connect to market-data providers through a backend so real-time flow and market data can be delivered securely.
Can I use WinnersFlow on mobile? +
Yes. The interface is designed mobile-first while retaining the denser terminal experience on larger screens.
Does WinnersFlow provide financial advice? +
No. The product is intended as a research and market-context tool. Users remain responsible for their own trading decisions.
